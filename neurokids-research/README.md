# NeuroKids Research

**https://freemanwd.github.io/sahvai/neurokids-research/**

A pediatric hydrocephalus research network site: Mayo Clinic Florida (coordinating center; Dr W. David Freeman, Dr Giselle Coelho), Boston Children's Hospital (Dr Benjamin Warf) and Santa Casa do Pará, Belém, Brazil (Dr Simone Rogério). It has an interactive globe and links to the [NeuroKids hydrocephalus measurement tool](https://freemanwd.github.io/sahvai/neurokids/).

We were inspired to build these education and research tools by [NeuroKids.org](https://neurokids.org/) and by Drs Giselle Coelho and Benjamin Warf. This site is independent of NeuroKids.org.

Design and globe adapted from the [SAHVAI Lab / SAHVERA site](https://github.com/freemanwd/sahvai) (d3-geo, topojson, Natural Earth via world-atlas; see `vendor/world-atlas-LICENSE.txt`).

Headshots and titles from the [STR-X Labs site](https://github.com/STR-X-Labs/str-x-labs.github.io).

Static site: open `index.html` or serve with `python3 -m http.server`.
